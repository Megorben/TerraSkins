TerraSkins - a wardrobe of purely cosmetic skins.

Open the wardrobe with the hanger button next to the builder toggles (inventory screen) or with the "Open Wardrobe" hotkey.
Pick a full outfit or build your own look slot by slot: head, body, legs, wings, capes and other accessory visuals, plus animated effects - auras (black holes, galaxies, divine rays...), halos, orbiting objects, trails and ground effects.

Every slot can use appearances from TerraSkins, from vanilla Terraria and from any other loaded mod.
Everything is visual only: no stats, no light, no gameplay advantage. Your look is synced in multiplayer.
